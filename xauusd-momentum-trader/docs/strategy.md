# Strategy Rules

## Objective

Gold momentum trades with:
- TP1: +7$
- TP2: +10$
- TP3: +15$
- SL: ~5$

## Entry Logic

### BUY
- H1 and 15m trend bullish
- Price above VWAP
- EMA20 above EMA50
- Breakout near important resistance
- Strong candle body
- Volume above recent average
- News risk low

### SELL
- H1 and 15m trend bearish
- Price below VWAP
- EMA20 below EMA50
- Breakdown near important support
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume above recent average
- News risk low

## Risk Rules

- No trade if score below 60
- Telegram only if score 75+
- Avoid high-impact news
- Move SL to breakeven around +5$
- Take partial profit before TP2
